About Us

Building a World Where She Can Thrive

SheCan Foundation was born from a simple but powerful belief — that no woman should be held back by circumstance. We work at the grassroots level, partnering with communities, schools, and local organisations to create lasting change.

From digital literacy to mental health support, our holistic approach ensures that every woman we reach gets the tools, confidence, and network she needs to succeed on her own terms.

What We Do

Programs That Create Change

01

Digital Sakhi

Teaching digital literacy and coding skills to women in rural India, opening doors to the tech economy.

02

Uday Scholarship

Annual scholarships for meritorious girls from low-income families to pursue higher education.

03

Rise & Lead

Leadership bootcamps and mentorship circles that equip women to take charge of their careers.

04

Safe Spaces

Mental health and legal aid support for survivors of gender-based violence and discrimination.

05

Craft to Commerce

Helping artisan women monetize traditional skills through e-commerce and market linkages.

06

HealthFirst

Awareness camps and free health screenings for women in peri-urban and rural communities.
